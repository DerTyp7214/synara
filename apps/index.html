Apps

The apps are free. They connect to a Synara server you run yourself, so start there if you don't have one yet.

Native apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and CarPlay are in the works.

Connect, log in, listen

Open the app, enter your server's address and log in.

After you log in

The app asks how this device should work with your others. You can change this any time in Settings.

  • Sync This Device

    • Sync Queue

      Keeps the play queue the same on all your devices. While a device is playing, changes from another device show up as a banner instead of being applied right away.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Desktop

    • Sync settings

      Keeps your preferences the same on every device signed in to this account.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Desktop

    • Sync scrobbler accounts

      Encrypts your ListenBrainz and Last.fm credentials with a passphrase before they leave the device. You use the same passphrase on every device.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Desktop

  • Remote control

    • Allow remote control

      Other devices signed in to your account can control playback on this device.

      Can be controlled
      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Apple TV
      • Android
      • Desktop
      Controls other devices
      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Apple Watch
      • Desktop

    • Lock screen controls

      The lock screen, Control Center and your headphone buttons show and control the device you're controlling. On the Mac this is called Media controls. On desktop the system media controls do the same (MPRIS on Linux, the Windows media controls, Now Playing on macOS).

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Desktop

  • Optional features

    • Podcasts

      Adds podcasts to Library and Home with their own player, kept apart from your music.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac
      • Apple Watch
      • Android
      • Desktop

    • Release Calendar

      Keeps upcoming releases in a dedicated Synara calendar and refreshes it daily.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Mac

    • Weekly and Monthly Recap

      Your top artist and track of the past week on Monday mornings, and of the past month on the 1st.

      • iPhone
      • iPad
      • Android

Demo content

The screenshots show the apps connected to a demo server. The music on it is licensed under Creative Commons, and these are the albums whose covers appear above.

  • Light Years by Stellardrone (2013), CC BY 4.0.
  • Directionless EP by Broke For Free (2011), CC BY 4.0.
  • Addressed to the Stars by Josh Woodward (2016), CC BY 4.0. The cover is the one that ships with the CC BY release.
  • Wry Way by Tale Twist (2016), CC BY 4.0.
  • Through the Lens by Andy G. Cohen (2015), CC BY 4.0. The cover is derived from ‘My World is Upside Down’ by Katelyn Kenderdine (CC BY 3.0). ‘Bumbler’ uses ‘playground 04’ by klankbeeld (CC BY 3.0).
  • Bunk by Jahzzar (2012), CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Not At All by Marco Trovatello (2018), CC BY 4.0. Cover by Rosa and Ada Trovatello. The fire sound on ‘Southfolk’ is by Mike Koenig (CC BY 3.0).
  • Blues For Peace by Hairy Larry and The Flying Hungarians (2008), CC BY 3.0.
  • Schumann: Kinderszenen, Op. 15 (Selections) by Gregory Tait (2020), CC BY 4.0. The cover uses William-Adolphe Bouguereau’s ‘Childhood Idyll’ (1900), which is in the public domain.
